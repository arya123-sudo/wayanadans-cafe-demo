Our Story

A little Wayanad,
in the heart of Peenya.

Wayanadan's Cafe brings the home kitchens of Wayanad to Pipeline Road — the district in Kerala's green north-east known for its pepper, its paddy fields and its unhurried, generous cooking.

Every day starts early here: puttu steaming by 7 AM for the first shift crowd, biryanis layered through the afternoon, and chai poured till midnight for the last bus home. It's the kind of food Bengaluru's Malayali community grew up on — and the kind the whole neighbourhood has now adopted.

With a 4.4 rating across 14,100+ Zomato delivery orders, the love is already proven. This page simply gives it a home of its own.
